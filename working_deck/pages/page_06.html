Proof of an Intelligent Microservice: The Petrophysical AI Agent

How an intelligent microservice automates high-stakes subsurface physics workflows—giving valuable time back to domain experts and increasing workflow decision accuracy.

01 CONTEXT // THE PETROPHYSICIST'S ROLE
Reservoir Characterization & Hydrocarbon Quantification

The Petrophysicist is the high-stakes domain expert tasked with interpreting porosity, permeability, and quantifying how much oil and gas is in the reservoir. This evaluation is one of the most critical decisions in oil & gas exploration and production.

02 THE BOTTLENECK // OPERATIONAL FRICTION & BUSINESS RISK
1–2 Hours of Manual Effort & Operational Risk

To quantify oil and accurately plan casing depths, separate wireline logging passes must be depth-matched and spliced. Splicing properly requires 1–2 hours of intensive manual effort. If done improperly or rushed at 3:00 AM, it introduces severe business risks—distorting reserve calculations and compromising casing depth integrity.

03 THE NEED // AN INTELLIGENT SYSTEM 3.2s EXECUTION
Physics Workflows: Cross-Correlation & Auto-Correction

An intelligent system that replicates the petrophysicist's physical workflow: runs signal cross-correlation on Gamma Ray over the overlap, calculates and applies the exact depth shift, normalizes tool recalibration drift, and presents an already-spliced composite log for 1-click human verification.
