## 1 · The Role

**One Line**: Interprets 2D and 3D seismic reflection volumes, calibrates borehole acoustic velocity measurements into depth domain maps, and performs quantitative attribute analysis to image subsurface structural traps, faults, and hydrocarbon fluid indicators.

**Why Their Output Matters**: The geophysicist's time-to-depth conversion maps define the exact subsurface coordinates, closure area, and gross rock volume (GRV) of prospective reservoirs. A minor 2.5% interval velocity error at a depth of 3,500 meters creates an **87-meter vertical depth error**, completely missing the crest of a structural trap, penetrating water-bearing limbs, or resulting in a catastrophic dry hole costing $25M–$100M+ in deepwater and frontier exploration.

**They do NOT Own:**
| Not Theirs | Whose |
|---|---|
| Microscopic Thin-Section Mineralogy, Core Porosity & Water Saturation Modeling | Petrophysicist (`P04`) |
| Basin Source Rock Geochemical Thermal Maturity & Petroleum System Modeling | Petroleum Geologist (`P05`) |
| High-Performance Computing Pre-Stack Depth Migration (PSDM) Processing Runs | Seismic Processing Contractor (CGG, SLB WesternGeco, TGS) |
| Wellbore Directional Trajectory Surveying & Anti-Collision Drilling Control | Directional Driller / Drilling Engineer (`P07`) |
| Reservoir Fluid Dynamic Simulation & Well Production Decline Surveillance | Reservoir Engineer (`P06`) |

**Variants**:
* **Structural Seismic Interpreter**: Focuses on complex tectonic regimes, fold-and-thrust belts, sub-salt imaging, regional fault network segmentation, and structural closure mapping.
* **Quantitative Interpretation (QI) / Rock Physics Geophysicist**: Focuses on rock physics diagnostics, Amplitude vs. Offset (AVO) fluid anomaly modeling, pre-stack acoustic/elastic impedance inversion, and 4D time-lapse seismic reservoir monitoring tracking fluid waterfronts.

**Title Check**: The industry canonical title is **Geophysicist**, **Seismic Interpreter**, or **Quantitative Interpretation (QI) Geophysicist**. Do not confuse with *"Petroleum Geologist"* (who focuses on depositional facies and stratigraphy, `P05`), *"Petrophysicist"* (who evaluates borehole wireline logs, `P04`), or *"Operations Geologist"* (who steers live wellbores at the rigsite, `P24`).

### Action Analysis (Two-Liners)

* **A01 · Checkshot Velocity Survey Quality Screening (B1, `[SEG-Standards §3]`)**:
  * *Today*: Geophysicist receives raw checkshot ASCII files; manually plots one-way travel time vs. depth in Excel to hunt for non-physical interval velocity spikes ($<1,500	ext{ m/s}$ or $>6,500	ext{ m/s}$) and transit time reversals.
  * *Failure Mode*: Unnoticed checkshot errors create artificial velocity pull-ups or push-downs, causing interpreters to map non-existent structural high targets.
  * *Agent Candidate*: **→ See §4 (Agent 1: Borehole Velocity Survey & Datum Harmonization Sieve)**.

* **A02 · Datum Reference Harmonization (B1, `[SEG-Standards §2]`)**:
  * *Today*: Wells in a basin reference Kelly Bushing (KB), ground level (GL), or mean sea level (MSL), while seismic references an arbitrary Seismic Reference Datum (SRD) with a replacement velocity $V_0$.
  * *Failure Mode*: Datum calculation errors ($15–30	ext{ meters}$) systematically shift synthetic seismograms, corrupting the entire regional depth conversion framework.
  * *Agent Candidate*: Integrated into Borehole Velocity Survey & Datum Harmonization Sieve (Agent 1).

* **A03 · Synthetic Seismogram Generation & Well-Ties (B1, `[Sheriff-Geldart §5]`)**:
  * *Today*: Interpreter manually loads sonic/density curves, extracts an estimated statistical wavelet, and clicks to stretch and squeeze synthetic reflections against seismic wiggle traces in Petrel GUIs.
  * *Failure Mode*: Over-stretching synthetics introduces non-geological velocity distortions, forcing artificial correlations that misidentify reservoir top reflection peaks.
  * *Agent Candidate*: **→ See §4 (Agent 2: Synthetic Seismogram & Multi-Well Time-to-Depth Well-Tie Sieve)**.

* **A04 · Horizon Tracking & Unconformity Mapping (B2, `[Sheriff-Geldart §8]`)**:

## 4 · Candidate Agent Deep-Dive

### Agent 1: Borehole Velocity Survey & Datum Harmonization Sieve
* **In One Line**: Ingests raw checkshot velocity tables, VSP reports, and sonic logs, screening out non-physical interval velocities, resolving elevation datum offsets (KB/GL/MSL/SRD), and computing calibrated time-depth functions.
* **Friction Solved**: Eliminates 7.25 hours of manual spreadsheet math, datum offset calculation, and velocity curve cleaning per multi-well prospect study.
* **The Specification**:
  * **Reads**: Raw checkshot velocity tables (measured depth vs. one-way travel time), Vertical Seismic Profile (VSP) reports, digital sonic wireline logs ($DT$), well header elevation metadata (Kelly Bushing elevation, Ground Level, Water Depth, Seismic Reference Datum [SRD], and replacement velocity $V_0$).
  * **Does**: Resolves datum shifts between wellhead reference and seismic reference datum; calculates interval velocities between survey receiver stations; detects non-physical interval velocities ($<1,400	ext{ m/s}$ in sedimentary rocks or $>7,000	ext{ m/s}$); identifies transit time reversals and knee-points; integrates sonic log travel times to compute sonic drift curves ($T_{	ext{checkshot}} - T_{	ext{sonic}}$); generates calibrated, monotonic time-depth ($T-Z$) tables.
  * **Returns**: Clean, quality-screened Time-Depth Calibration Dataset with drift correction curves, flagged station anomalies, and direct export files formatted for Petrel, DecisionSpace, and Kingdom.
  * **Stops At**: Overwriting raw proprietary wireline contractor archive files or altering physical well header survey records.
* **Failure Modes & Safety Envelopes**: If checkshot survey points exhibit severe velocity inversions ($>500	ext{ m/s}$ drop in interval velocity over a 100-meter interval), the agent flags `[Velocity Inversion Detected: Salt / Overpressure / Gas Zone Review Required]` and highlights the exact depth interval rather than automatically smoothing it out.

### Agent 2: Synthetic Seismogram & Multi-Well Time-to-Depth Well-Tie Sieve
* **In One Line**: Convolves calibrated acoustic impedance logs with extracted statistical or deterministic wavelets and performs sliding-window cross-correlation against seismic traces to generate stretch-squeeze tie proposals.
* **Friction Solved**: Removes 10.5 hours of tedious manual click-and-drag stretching of synthetic reflection peaks against seismic wiggle traces in interpretation software.
* **The Specification**:
  * **Reads**: Calibrated compressional sonic logs ($DT$), bulk density logs ($RHOB$), calibrated time-depth curves from Agent 1, and extracted 3D seismic trace corridors around the borehole trajectory.
  * **Does**: Computes acoustic impedance ($AI = V_p 	imes ho$); calculates primary reflection coefficient series ($RC$); estimates wavelet bandwidth and phase rotation from seismic; convolves reflection coefficients with the extracted wavelet to generate the synthetic seismogram; executes a multi-scale sliding-window cross-correlation against the seismic trace corridor; optimizes time-shifts and constrained stretch/squeeze factors ($\le \pm 5\%$ velocity change limit) to maximize cross-correlation coefficients ($R$).
  * **Returns**: Automated Well-Tie Evaluation Dossier featuring aligned synthetic-to-seismic comparison tracks, cross-correlation match scores ($R$), residual phase rotation estimates ($\Delta 	heta$), and recommended stretch-squeeze control point tables.
  * **Stops At**: Applying non-geological velocity stretches exceeding $\pm 10\%$ without explicit geophysicist review and authorization.
* **Failure Modes & Safety Envelopes**: If maximum cross-correlation coefficient $R < 0.60$ across the reservoir target interval, the agent flags `[Poor Well-Tie Quality: Phase Rotation, Wavelet Distortion, or Fault Cut Suspected]` and isolates the depth discrepancy for human expert diagnosis.

### Agent 3: Multi-Layer Velocity Model & Depth Conversion Uncertainty Sieve
* **In One Line**: Fits layer-specific velocity functions ($V_0 + kZ$, Voigt, or Dix interval velocities) across calibrated well-ties and executes Monte Carlo depth conversion simulations to quantify reservoir gross rock volume (GRV) uncertainty.
* **Friction Solved**: Eliminates 14.0 hours of manual spreadsheet regression fitting, velocity gridding, and manual sensitivity case running per regional exploration study.
* **The Specification**:
  * **Reads**: Two-way travel time (TWT) interpreted horizon surfaces, calibrated well-tie depth markers, formation top markers, and regional velocity survey datasets.
  * **Does**: Segments the subsurface into geological velocity layers (e.g., Post-Salt Overburden, Massive Carbonate, Target Sand, Basal Shale); performs linear regression to establish $V_0 + kZ$ instantaneous velocity functions for each layer; identifies lateral velocity anomalies (e.g., gas sags, carbonate reef velocity pull-ups, salt dome flanks); calculates layer-cake depth surfaces; executes Monte Carlo uncertainty simulations perturbing velocity parameters within measured well variance bounds ($P_{10}, P_{50}, P_{90}$).
  * **Returns**: Depth Conversion Framework Package containing calibrated velocity parameter tables, structural depth maps for all reservoir horizons, and probabilistic Gross Rock Volume (GRV) uncertainty curves ($P_{10}/P_{50}/P_{90}$).
  * **Stops At**: Arbitrarily altering geological horizon picks or forcing depth surfaces through inaccurate well tops without velocity documentation.
* **Failure Modes & Safety Envelopes**: If depth conversion at any blind calibration well produces a depth residual exceeding $\pm 2.0\%$ of true vertical depth, the agent flags `[Velocity Model Residual Out-of-Tolerance: Lateral Velocity Gradient Unaccounted For]` and identifies the outlying well location.

### Agent 4: Vintage 2D/3D Seismic Survey Harmonization & Phase/Amplitude Sieve
* **In One Line**: Harmonizes multi-vintage 2D lines and 3D seismic surveys by cross-correlating trace overlaps to resolve static time shifts, phase rotations, and amplitude scalar imbalances.
* **Friction Solved**: Eliminates 14.0 hours of tedious manual mistie measurement and spreadsheet tracking across disparate exploration survey vintages.
* **The Specification**:
  * **Reads**: Overlapping 2D seismic lines and 3D seismic survey volumes, navigation coordinates, trace header metadata (sample rate, polarity, filter settings), and intersection line profiles.
  * **Does**: Analyzes seismic trace intersections across survey boundaries; computes static travel-time misties ($\Delta t$ in milliseconds); calculates residual phase rotation angles ($\Delta 	heta$ in degrees) using spectral phase unwrapping; balances root-mean-square (RMS) amplitude levels across surveys to normalize reflection strengths; solves a global least-squares network mistie matrix to distribute residual closures evenly.
  * **Returns**: Seismic Survey Mistie Harmonization Matrix containing static time shifts, phase correction angles, and amplitude scalar coefficients for every input survey, ready for direct batch application.
  * **Stops At**: Overwriting original raw processed seismic trace files or applying non-linear trace interpolation.
* **Failure Modes & Safety Envelopes**: If intersection cross-correlation between two surveys falls below $0.50$ (indicating completely incompatible bandwidth or severe navigation positioning errors), the agent flags `[Survey Intersection Incompatible: Spatial Navigation or Processing Mismatch]` and excludes the line intersection from the global adjustment network.

### Agent 5: AVO Pre-Stack Attribute & Fluid Anomaly Sieve
* **In One Line**: Evaluates conditioned pre-stack angle gathers along reservoir horizons to compute AVO intercept ($A$) and gradient ($B$), screen for fluid anomalies, and classify hydrocarbon sand signatures.
* **Friction Solved**: Removes 8.75 hours of manual gather extraction, super-gather binning, and cross-plot cluster isolation per prospect evaluation.
* **The Specification**:
  * **Reads**: Pre-stack seismic angle gathers (near, mid, far, ultra-far offsets), interpreted reservoir top/base horizons, and background rock physics elastic property trends ($V_p/V_s$ vs. acoustic impedance).
  * **Does**: Performs automated residual moveout (RMO) flatness quality checks on angle gathers; computes AVO intercept ($A$) and gradient ($B$) using Shuey's two-term approximation; generates $A 	imes B$ product, scaled Poisson ratio change, and fluid factor ($\Delta F$) attribute maps; establishes background brine-saturated rock trend lines on $A$-vs-$B$ cross-plots; identifies and clusters anomalous data points deviating from background trend (Rutherford-Williams Class I, II, III, or IV gas sand signatures).
  * **Returns**: AVO Prospect Fluid Anomaly Dossier featuring intercept-gradient cross-plots, classified AVO anomaly polygon maps, and quantitative fluid confidence scores.
  * **Stops At**: Classifying an amplitude anomaly as an economic hydrocarbon discovery without petrophysical and rock physics shear-wave calibration.
* **Failure Modes & Safety Envelopes**: If angle gathers exhibit severe residual moveout ($>8	ext{ ms}$ over 30 degrees angle of incidence) or significant phase distortion, the agent issues an `[AVO Gather Distortion Warning: Velocity Moveout Unflattened]` flag to prevent false-positive DHI (Direct Hydrocarbon Indicator) interpretation.

### Agent 6: Seismic Structural Fault Seal & Juxtaposition Sieve
* **In One Line**: Extracts fault throw distributions along interpreted 3D fault planes and integrates petrophysical clay volume ($V_{\text{sh}}$) curves to map stratigraphic juxtaposition and calculate Shale Gouge Ratio (SGR) seal integrity.
* **Friction Solved**: Eliminates 7.0 hours per prospect of manual fault slicing, horizon cutoff measurement, and fault seal spreadsheet construction.
* **The Specification**:
  * **Reads**: 3D structural fault sticks and triangulated fault surfaces, interpreted hanging wall and footwall horizon grids, and offset well petrophysical clay volume ($V_{\text{shale}}$) wireline logs.
  * **Does**: Traces stratigraphic cutoffs across mapped fault planes; computes along-fault throw profiles and fault slip vectors; detects fault throw anomalies (sudden throw drops indicating correlation errors); evaluates footwall-hanging wall stratigraphic juxtaposition (identifying sand-on-sand leak windows vs. sand-on-shale seals); calculates along-fault Shale Gouge Ratio ($SGR = \sum [V_{\text{shale}} \times \Delta z] / \text{Throw}$); predicts maximum supported hydrocarbon column height based on capillary entry pressure correlations.

## 7 · Lines Worth Remembering & Open Questions

### The Line
> *"A geophysicist's true intellectual value is knowing whether a bright seismic reflection is a massive gas reservoir or just a porous brine sand—not spending three days in Excel figuring out whether an old discovery well referenced Kelly Bushing or ground level."*

### Authentic Open Questions for Geophysics Practitioners
1. **The Well-Tie Distortion Risk**: When preparing multi-well assets for seismic inversion, how often do interpreters over-stretch synthetic seismograms by $\pm 15\%$ to force a visual match with seismic wiggles, unknowingly introducing false non-physical interval velocity layers into the depth conversion model?
2. **The Survey Mistie Blind Spot**: Across your exploration blocks combining 2D lines and multiple 3D vintages, how many "structural faults" mapped in the subsurface are actually uncorrected static time misties or 90-degree phase rotations between adjacent seismic acquisition contracts?
3. **The Depth Conversion Margin of Error**: What is the historical average depth discrepancy between your team's pre-drill seismic depth prognosis and the actual formation top encountered by the drilling bit—and what percentage of that variance was driven by unmodeled lateral velocity gradients in the overburden?
