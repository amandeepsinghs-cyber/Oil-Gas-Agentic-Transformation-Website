## 0 · Status

### Headline Finding
> **The Retail Territory Manager is the commercial and operational linchpin of the downstream fuels marketing network, ensuring forecourt wetstock integrity, statutory environmental and safety compliance, and dealer margin realization across dozens of retail outlets.**
> While back-office retail management platforms like **Gilbarco Veeder-Root Insite360**, **Titan Cloud**, **Kalibrate**, and **SAP Secondary Distribution** host automated tank gauge telemetry, electronic point-of-sale (POS) feeds, and price book distribution, **they function as passive, disconnected reporting repositories that leave operational cross-referencing stranded across spreadsheets, dealer phone calls, and manual site visits**. A territory manager overseeing 35 retail service stations spends up to 2.5 hours every morning manually comparing underground automated tank gauge (ATG) dips against dispenser point-of-sale totalizers to catch meter drift or underground leaks, re-calculating multi-compartment delivery dispatch plans against safe-fill ullage, and sifting through paper forecourt inspection logs. A single undetected slow dispenser meter drift (e.g. 0.4% beyond legal tolerance) across a busy multi-product dispenser bleeds thousands of liters in unaccounted giveaway each month, while missed environmental inspection items risk immediate statutory shutdown by safety authorities. A hyper-specialized 7-agent squad eliminates **9.70 hours per operational cycle** across automated wetstock statistical reconciliation, predictive forecourt replenishment planning, mobile HSSE audit remediation, dispenser meter proving verification, retail pricing execution audits, and dealer business review synthesis.

## 1 · The Role

**One Line**: Governs operational integrity, statutory environmental and safety compliance, wetstock inventory reconciliation, and commercial profitability across a multi-site network of retail fuel stations.

**Why Their Output Matters**: A failure in territory forecourt management leads directly to undetected underground fuel leaks contaminating municipal aquifers and incurring catastrophic environmental remediation liabilities, uncalibrated dispenser meters giving away millions of liters of product or cheating motorists and triggering statutory seal-offs by Weights and Measures inspectors, dry forecourts losing market share during delivery stock-outs, or catastrophic flash fires and vapor explosions caused by neglected electrical grounding, leaking shear valves, or improper road tanker fuel decantation.

**They do NOT Own:**
| Not Theirs | Whose |
|---|---|
| Bulk Fuel Depot Gantry Automation, Pipeline Injection & Bulk Storage Tank Operations | Terminal Superintendent (`P15`) / Terminal Operations Manager |
| Secondary Distribution Fleet Hauler Dispatch & Road Tanker Maintenance | Bulk Logistics Fleet Dispatcher / Transport Contractor |
| Refinery Downstream Production Planning & LP Crude Economics | Production Planner (`P17`) / Refinery Scheduler |
| Forecourt Electronic Dispenser Hydraulic & Submersible Turbine Pump Mechanical Overhauls | Authorized Petroleum Equipment Service Contractor (PEI Certified Tech) |
| Commercial B2B Industrial Fleet Wholesale Fuel Contracts & Marine Bunkering | Commercial Fuels & Lubricants Account Manager |

**Variants**:
* **Company-Owned, Company-Operated (COCO) Territory Manager**: Directly supervises salaried station managers and forecourt customer service attendants; controls site labor rosters, cash reconciliation, store merchandising, and company branding standards.
* **Company-Owned, Dealer-Operated (CODO) / Dealer-Owned, Dealer-Operated (DODO) Territory Manager**: Operates in franchise oversight mode; audits independent dealer compliance with franchise agreements, credit limits, exclusive supply covenants, brand standards, and statutory HSSE requirements.
* **Highway & Travel Plaza Territory Manager**: Oversees high-throughput mega-stations featuring high-flow satellite diesel dispensers for heavy commercial vehicles, extensive convenience store retail, fast-food franchises, and multi-product CNG/EV charging infrastructure.

**Title Check**: The industry title is **Retail Territory Manager**, **Territory Manager - Retail**, or **Forecourt Operations Specialist**. Avoid confusing this role with *"C-Store Category Manager"* (who manages retail packaged goods, snacks, and vendor supply contracts from a corporate desk) or *"Station Manager"* (who resides at a single physical retail outlet managing shift pump attendants).

### Agent 1: Daily Wetstock Reconciliation & SIR Sieve
* **In One Line**: Ingests automated tank gauge (ATG) dips, dispenser point-of-sale (POS) delivery journals, and fuel temperature telemetry to execute API RP 1621 variance reconciliation and statistical inventory leak detection.
* **Friction Solved**: Eliminates 2.10 hours per day of manual spreadsheet compilation, isolating true underground tank leaks and dispenser meter drift from harmless thermal fuel expansion.
* **The Specification**:
  * **Reads**: Automated tank gauge (ATG) hourly inventory logs (product level, water cut, fuel temperature), electronic POS dispenser pump transactions, delivery drop receipts, and tank calibration strapping charts.
  * **Does**: Normalizes fuel volumes to 15 degrees Celsius (or 60 degrees Fahrenheit) using standard petroleum measurement tables; solves daily and 30-day rolling cumulative inventory variances: Variance = (Closing Stock + Sales) - (Opening Stock + Deliveries); compares calculated variance against API RP 1621 thresholds (0.5% of throughput + 50 gallons) and EPA 40 CFR 280 statistical inventory reconciliation (SIR) standards (0.2 gal/hr leak detection limit); isolates sudden level drops indicative of underground leaks from gradual systematic negative trends indicative of meter drift or temperature shrinkage.
  * **Returns**: Daily Wetstock Integrity Ledger (station_id, tank_id, product_type, gross_variance_liters, net_variance_liters, variance_pct, leak_risk_score, meter_drift_flag).
  * **Stops At**: Declaring a statutory environmental leak emergency, ordering immediate station tank shutdown, or filing regulatory spill notifications.
* **Failure Modes & Safety Envelopes**: If ATG probe water-level reading jumps by more than 25 mm within a single 2-hour window, the agent issues an emergency override banner: [Sudden Water Ingress Alert — Ingress Hazard Detected — Cease Dispensing Immediately].

### Agent 2: Forecourt Replenishment & Ullage Dispatch Optimizer
* **In One Line**: Evaluates real-time underground tank ullage, product sales burn-rates, and road tanker turnaround times to generate predictive multi-compartment fuel delivery schedules.
* **Friction Solved**: Eliminates 1.25 hours per day of phone-based delivery coordination and emergency re-routing, preventing forecourt dry runs and tanker turnaways.
* **The Specification**:
  * **Reads**: Real-time ATG inventory levels, safe-fill capacity limits (typically 90% or 95% of gross tank volume), 7-day hourly sales velocity profiles, local weather/event forecasts, road tanker compartment sizes (e.g. 5,000L to 9,000L compartments), and depot loading rack queue times.
  * **Does**: Calculates time-to-runout (TTR) for each product grade across all territory forecourts; calculates available safe-fill ullage; models optimal multi-compartment truck drop combinations that satisfy full-truckload delivery economics without breaching safe-fill margins; sequences drops to minimize road transit mileage and avoid peak urban traffic windows.
  * **Returns**: Territory Fuel Replenishment Manifest (order_id, station_id, product_grade, recommended_drop_liters, target_delivery_window, available_ullage_liters, stockout_risk_score).
  * **Stops At**: Authorizing official financial purchase orders or committing corporate credit lines with transport haulers.
* **Failure Modes & Safety Envelopes**: If projected delivery volume exceeds 92% of calculated safe-fill ullage at estimated delivery arrival time, the agent flags an automatic hold: [Overfill Prevention Lock — Safe-Fill Margin Violated — Adjust Compartment Allocation].

* **Friction Solved**: Eliminates 1.65 hours per station inspection of transcribing handwritten notes, tracking open safety non-conformances, and chasing dealer maintenance remediation.
* **The Specification**:
  * **Reads**: Territory manager mobile inspection photos (dispenser islands, containment sumps, flame arrestors, electrical switchboards), digital audit checklists, fire extinguisher inspection tags, emergency stop (ESD) testing logs, and station operating licenses (PESO / municipal fire permits).
  * **Does**: Evaluates inspection data against PEI RP500/RP900 and OISD-STD-141 safety clauses; flags expired statutory permits, missing fire bucket sand, obstructed emergency shutoff switches, waterlogged under-dispenser sumps, or compromised impact shear valves; grades stations on a standardized 100-point HSSE compliance index; assigns priority remediation deadlines (24-hour critical vs. 14-day non-critical).

### Agent 4: Dispenser Meter Calibration & Legal Metrology Sieve
* **In One Line**: Analyzes 5-liter and 20-liter proving measure can calibration logs, Weights and Measures stamping certificates, and electronic pump totalizer ratios to detect nozzle calibration drift and prevent commercial giveaway.
* **Friction Solved**: Eliminates 1.25 hours per week of manual calibration calculation and meter ledger audits, catching subtle dispenser over-dispensing before regulatory seal-off.
* **The Specification**:
  * **Reads**: Manual 5L/20L proving measure can test runs, nozzle electronic pulsar counts, dispenser totalizer readings, legal metrology stamping expiry dates, and fuel operating temperatures.
  * **Does**: Computes percentage meter delivery error: Error (%) = ((V_dispensed - V_proving_can) / V_proving_can) * 100; compares calculated error against national Legal Metrology / OIML R 117 Maximum Permissible Error (MPE) thresholds (typically plus/minus 0.3% to 0.5%); calculates monetary revenue loss or consumer short-measure exposure per nozzle based on 30-day sales volume; flags impending stamping expirations 30 days in advance.
  * **Returns**: Dispenser Calibration Health Ledger (station_id, dispenser_id, nozzle_id, product_grade, measured_error_pct, metrology_status, giveaway_volume_30d_liters, stamping_validity_date).
  * **Stops At**: Breaking official Weights and Measures lead wire seals or adjusting mechanical calibrator screws on dispenser meters.
* **Failure Modes & Safety Envelopes**: If any nozzle exhibits an under-delivery error exceeding -0.5% (short-measuring consumers), the agent triggers an immediate alert: [Legal Metrology Non-Compliance — High Risk of Statutory Prosecution — Tag Nozzle Out of Service].
