## 1 · The Role

**One Line**: Governs statutory environmental compliance, emissions accounting, and regulatory reporting for operating energy facilities, ensuring fugitive methane, flare gas volumes, criteria air pollutants ($SO_x, NO_x, CO, VOC$), and effluent discharges remain strictly within legal permit envelopes.

**Why Their Output Matters**: A failure in environmental compliance results in catastrophic corporate consequences: statutory shutdown orders from environmental protection agencies, multi-million-dollar civil enforcement fines (e.g., EPA Clean Air Act settlements exceeding $20,000,000 for systemic LDAR non-compliance), loss of social license to operate, and criminal liabilities for falsified or late emissions filings. Furthermore, unmonitored flaring and fugitive leaks vent valuable natural gas straight into the atmosphere, destroying corporate carbon neutrality and methane intensity targets.

**They do NOT Own:**
| Not Theirs | Whose |
|---|---|
| Process Safety Incident Root Cause Analysis and HAZOP Safety Cases | Process Safety Engineer / Process Safety Lead |
| Mechanical Replacement of Leaking Valve Packings, Flange Gaskets, and Seals | Plant Maintenance Technician / Machinist |
| Direct Control Room DCS Flare Header Pressure and Steam Injection Tuning | Board Operator / Console Operator |
| Corporate Environmental ESG Public Relations and Investor Advertising | Corporate Communications / Investor Relations |
| Occupational Safety and Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Compliance | Occupational Health & Safety Specialist |

**Variants**:
* **Air Quality & Emissions Specialist**: Focuses heavily on Title V air permits, Continuous Emission Monitoring Systems (CEMS) on combustion turbines and furnaces, flare combustion efficiency, and statutory greenhouse gas inventories (EPA Subpart W).
* **Field LDAR & Compliance Lead**: Specializes in Leak Detection and Repair (LDAR) field execution, coordinating Optical Gas Imaging (OGI) infrared camera inspections, Method 21 FID sniffer testing, component tag management, and Delay of Repair (DOR) governance.
* **Water & Waste Compliance Specialist**: Manages National Pollutant Discharge Elimination System (NPDES) industrial wastewater permits, produced water re-injection/discharge, stormwater run-off, SPCC secondary containment, and hazardous waste manifests (RCRA).

**Title Check**: The industry title is **Environmental Specialist**, **Emissions Engineer**, or **Environmental Compliance Lead**. Avoid confusing with *"Process Safety Engineer"* (who focuses on preventing acute catastrophic vessel ruptures and fires) or *"Occupational Safety Specialist"* (who focuses on worker slips, trips, falls, and PPE).

### Agent 2: EPA Subpart W & Quad O Methane Flaring & Combustion Efficiency Sieve
* **In One Line**: Correlates flare header ultrasonic flowmeters, gas chromatograph compositions, pilot thermocouples, and steam/air assist rates to compute net heating value ($NHV_{cz}$), dynamic destruction efficiency ($DRE$), and uncombusted methane slip.
* **Friction Solved**: Removes 8.75 hours per month of manual meter data reconciliation, un-combusted methane slip calculation, and flaring event root-cause investigation.
* **The Specification**:
  * **Reads**: Flare header ultrasonic flowmeters, fuel gas composition analyzers, pilot burner thermocouple telemetry, steam/air assist flow rates, and operating unit trip logs.
  * **Does**: Reconciles raw flare telemetry using mass balance verification; calculates net heating value in the combustion zone ($NHV_{cz}$) per EPA 40 CFR 63.670:
    $$NHV_{cz} = \frac{Q_{vg} NHV_{vg} + Q_{aux} NHV_{aux}}{Q_{vg} + Q_{aux} + Q_{assist}}$$
    verifying compliance with statutory threshold ($NHV_{cz} \ge 270\text{ Btu/sc}$); calculates dynamic destruction and removal efficiency ($DRE \ge 98\%$); quantifies flare gas emissions ($CO_2, CH_4, N_2O, SO_x$) per API GHG Compendium methodologies; detects unlit flare venting events when gas flow exists but pilot thermocouples show flameout ($T_{pilot} < 200^\circ\text{C}$); aggregates unit flaring volumes into statutory reporting formats (EPA Subpart W, OISD).
  * **Returns**: Monthly certified flare emissions ledger, unlit flare venting anomaly alerts, steam/air over-assist loss calculations, and pre-formatted greenhouse gas inventory tables.
  * **Stops At**: Adjusting flare tip steam control valves or altering refinery pressure relief systems.
* **Failure Modes & Safety Envelopes**: If flare flow is detected while all pilot thermocouples indicate loss of flame, the agent issues an immediate plant emergency alert: `[UNLIT FLARE VENTING DETECTED: High-Volume Raw Hydrocarbon/Methane Discharge to Atmosphere — Immediate Pilot Re-Ignition Required]`.

### Agent 4: Continuous Emissions Monitoring System (CEMS) RATA & Drift Sieve
