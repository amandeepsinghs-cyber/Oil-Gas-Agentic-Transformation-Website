## 1 · The Role

**One Line**: Administers natural gas transportation, storage, and gathering agreements, enforces pipeline tariff and open-access compliance, evaluates secondary capacity release transactions, monitors counterparty credit exposure, and prepares certified regulatory customer index and rate filings.

**Why Their Output Matters**: The Gas Contracts Analyst safeguards the commercial viability, cash flow integrity, and legal standing of the pipeline transmission business. A missed tariff constraint, an inaccurate Maximum Daily Quantity (MDQ) contract record, or an overlooked secondary capacity release condition allows shippers to lean on pipeline linepack without paying reservation fees, triggers unlawful preferential capacity allocation, or results in catastrophic uncollateralized credit losses when an over-extended shipper defaults on monthly transportation and cash-out liabilities. Furthermore, submitting delayed or inaccurate customer index disclosures triggers immediate regulatory enforcement actions, mandatory customer rate refunds, and severe financial penalties.

**They do NOT Own:**
| Not Theirs | Whose |
|---|---|
| Physical Supervisory Control, Compressor Station Remote Control, and Pipeline Pressure Dispatch | Pipeline Controller (`P14`) / Gas Control Operator (`P42`) |
| Daily and Intraday Pipeline Nominations Scheduling and Confirmation Matching | Pipeline Gas Scheduler |
| Commercial Negotiation of Long-Term Foundation Shipper Precedent Agreements and Capital Expansion Tariffs | Commercial Vice President / Director of Business Development |
| Custody Transfer Meter Proving, Flow Computer Calibration, and Gas Chromatograph Maintenance | Gas Measurement Specialist / Measurement Technician |
| Formal Statutory Rate Case Litigation and Legal Dispute Proceedings Before Regulatory Commissions | Regulatory Affairs Director / General Counsel |

**Variants**:
* **Transportation & Capacity Release Specialist**: Focuses heavily on daily capacity release auctions, pre-arranged release validation, recall and reput rights, biddable reservation rate ceilings, replacement shipper credit qualification, and secondary market Electronic Bulletin Board (EBB) administration under NAESB WGQ Quadrant 5.
* **Midstream Contracts Administrator**: Specializes in ingesting, abstracting, and executing long-term Firm Transportation (FT), Interruptible Transportation (IT), Firm Storage Service (FSS), and Gathering Agreements; tracking evergreen contract rollovers, Right of First Refusal (ROFR) notification windows, primary receipt and delivery meter entitlements, and contract amendments.
* **Commercial Regulatory & Settlement Analyst**: Manages monthly shipper physical-versus-scheduled imbalance reconciliation, tiered tariff cash-out calculations, operational balancing agreements (OBAs), system fuel retention percentage audits, and statutory filings including FERC Form 549B (Index of Customers).

**Title Check**: The canonical industry title is **Gas Contracts Analyst**, **Gas Contracts Administrator**, or **Transportation Contracts Specialist**. Do not confuse with *"Pipeline Scheduler"* (who manages daily nomination cycle confirmation matching and point scheduling cuts) or *"Gas Trader"* (who buys and sells physical gas molecules and financial basis swaps).

## 2 · Sources & Competency Standards

### Sponsoring Competency Standards
1. `[NAESB-WGQ]`: North American Energy Standards Board, *Wholesale Gas Quadrant (WGQ) Standards*, Version 3.2 — Standard 6.3.1 (Base Contract for Sale and Purchase of Natural Gas), Quadrant 1 (Nominations & Confirmations), Quadrant 3 (Invoicing & Financial Settlement), and Quadrant 5 (Capacity Release Standards).
2. `[FERC-Part-284]`: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, *18 CFR Part 284 — Certain Sales and Transportation of Natural Gas Under the Natural Gas Policy Act and Open Access Transportation* (§284.7 Firm and interruptible transportation rates, §284.12 Standards for pipeline business practices, §284.13 Information to be posted on EBB, and §284.106/§284.126 Form 549B Index of Customers).
3. `[PNGRB-Access-Code]`: Petroleum and Natural Gas Regulatory Board (India), *Access Code for Common Carrier or Contract Carrier Natural Gas Pipelines Regulations (2008 & 2020 Amendments)* and *Guiding Principles for Declaring/Authorising Natural Gas Pipelines* (Open-access capacity allocation, Gas Transmission Agreements [GTA], imbalance settlement, and capacity disclosure).
4. `[EU-CAM-NC]`: European Commission, *Regulation (EU) 2017/459 — Network Code on Capacity Allocation Mechanisms in Gas Transmission Networks (CAM NC)* and *Regulation (EU) No 312/2014 (Balancing Network Code)* (Third-party open access capacity allocation, standardized capacity products, and commercial balancing).
5. `[ISO-15112]`: International Organization for Standardization, *ISO 15112: Natural Gas — Energy Determination* and *ISO 13686: Natural Gas — Quality Designation* (Energy billing calculations, gross calorific value specifications, and custody transfer energy measurement).

### Action Analysis (Two-Liners)

* **A01 · Service Agreement Ingestion & Contract Parameter Abstraction (B1, `[NAESB-WGQ §6.3.1]`)**:
  * *Today*: Analyst spends 4 to 6 hours manually reviewing 30-to-80 page executed Transportation Service Agreements (TSAs), transcribing primary meter pins, Maximum Daily Quantities (MDQ/MHQ), reservation rates, and fuel tier matrix schedules into TMS database tables.
  * *Failure Mode*: Manual transcription typos in receipt meter pin numbers or fuel tier schedules result in incorrect monthly reservation invoices, invalid nomination rejections, and costly customer billing disputes.
  * *Agent Candidate*: **→ See §4 (Agent 1: Service Agreement Abstract & Entitlement Sieve)**.

* **A02 · Monthly Reservation & Commodity Billing Computation (B2, `[FERC-Part-284 §284.7]`)**:
  * *Today*: Executed inside commercial transportation management systems (Quorum TIPS / W Energy). The software multiplies active contract MDQ reservation determinants by approved tariff rates and applies commodity charges to scheduled deliveries.
  * *Failure Mode*: Standard deterministic calculation within the commercial billing engine; formula execution belongs to monolith transportation billing software.
  * *Agent Candidate*: `❌ No`. Monolith TMS billing engine.

* **A03 · Capacity Release Bidding & Tariff Rule Evaluation (B1, `[NAESB-WGQ Quadrant 5]`)**:
  * *Today*: When releasing shippers post capacity offers, the analyst manually verifies biddable rates against maximum tariff ceilings, checks recall terms against standard operational cycles, validates replacement shipper creditworthiness, and calculates Net Present Value (NPV) tie-breakers in complex spreadsheets.

## 4 · Candidate Agent Deep-Dive

### Agent 1: Service Agreement Abstract & Entitlement Sieve
* **In One Line**: Ingests executed natural gas transportation, storage, and gathering agreements to extract commercial terms, primary path rights, Maximum Daily Quantities (MDQ/MHQ), and billing formulas into enterprise contract repositories.
* **Friction Solved**: Eliminates 12.0 hours per month of manual multi-page PDF contract parsing, clause interpretation, and repetitive data entry into pipeline transportation management systems.
* **The Specification**:
  * **Reads**: Executed transportation service agreements (FT, IT), firm storage agreements (FSS, ISS), gathering agreements, interconnect agreements, contract exhibits, rate schedules, and legal amendments.
  * **Does**: Parses structured and unstructured PDF contracts; extracts key commercial metadata including shipper legal entity, contract type, execution/effective/termination dates, rollover/evergreen notice windows, primary receipt and delivery meter pins, Maximum Daily Quantity (MDQ in Dth/d or GJ/d), Maximum Hourly Quantity (MHQ), primary path routing, reservation rates ($/Dth/month), commodity rates, fuel retention matrix tiers, and overrun penalty formulas; validates extracted parameters against active pipeline tariff rate bands; formats data into native TMS ingestion schemas (Quorum TIPS / W Energy / SAP).
  * **Returns**: Contract Ingestion & Abstraction Dossier with structured parameter tables, side-by-side verification view with source PDF snippet highlights, evergreen expiration schedule alerts, and tariff discrepancy flags.
  * **Stops At**: Authorizing deviations from published open-access pipeline tariff rates or committing unexecuted contract drafts.
* **Failure Modes & Safety Envelopes**: If contract terms contain non-standard negotiated clauses (e.g., custom pressure commitments, lateral construction reimbursement offsets, or non-conforming indemnities) that deviate from pro forma tariff service agreements, the agent flags `[Non-Conforming Tariff Clause Detected: Legal & Regulatory Review Required]` and suppresses automated TMS sync until commercial counsel approves.

### Agent 2: Capacity Release Bidding & Tariff Compliance Sieve
* **In One Line**: Evaluates secondary market capacity release postings, pre-arranged transactions, and incoming competitive bids against tariff rate ceilings, bidding timelines, credit prequalification, and recall conditions.
* **Friction Solved**: Eliminates 11.5 hours per month of manual bid evaluation, net present value (NPV) tie-breaker calculations, tariff maximum rate validation, and replacement shipper eligibility verification.
* **The Specification**:
  * **Reads**: Capacity release offer postings (releasing shipper ID, contract number, quantity in Dth/d, primary path, release term, minimum acceptable bid rate, recall/reput rights), competitive electronic bids, pipeline tariff rate schedule limits, and replacement shipper credit status tables.
  * **Does**: Audits release postings against regulatory timelines (NAESB WGQ Quadrant 5 bidding windows based on release duration); verifies proposed bid rates against maximum tariff reservation rate caps (or flags short-term release exemption under FERC Order 712 / market-based caps); calculates bid economic value using tariff-specified Net Present Value (NPV) formulas for tie-breaking; verifies recall/reput conditions against standard operational notice requirements; confirms replacement shipper satisfies tariff creditworthiness prequalification; prepares award notifications for Electronic Bulletin Board (EBB) publication.
  * **Returns**: Capacity Release Evaluation & Award Dossier with calculated bid NPV scores, tariff compliance verification tags, credit prequalification clearance status, and ready-to-post electronic award summaries.
  * **Stops At**: Declaring official capacity release auction winners or executing contract assignments on the pipeline EBB without commercial analyst sign-off.
* **Failure Modes & Safety Envelopes**: If a winning bid proposes a rate exceeding the statutory maximum tariff rate on a long-term release (> 1 year), or if a pre-arranged release rate is below maximum tariff rate and requires mandatory competitive bidding but is submitted without the required public bidding window, the agent issues `[Tariff Rate Ceiling / Bidding Window Violation]` and halts award processing.

  * **Does**: Validates nominated volumes against contract MDQ limits; verifies whether requested receipt and delivery point combinations follow contract primary paths or constitute secondary out-of-path nominations; audits segmentation rights to ensure multiple segment nominations do not exceed contract MDQ in any single physical pipeline segment; flags nominations exceeding Maximum Hourly Quantity (MHQ) flow rates; cross-references active OFO restrictions to identify unauthorized deliveries or non-compliant receipts during emergency operational alerts.

  * **Stops At**: Directly executing pipeline nomination scheduling cuts, issuing physical confirmation rejections, or altering operator flow orders.
* **Failure Modes & Safety Envelopes**: If a shipper's nomination exceeds contract MDQ during a critical Operational Flow Order (OFO) or Critical Period alert, the agent flags `[Critical OFO Over-Nomination: Unauthorized Overrun Penalty Alert]` and immediately alerts the contracts analyst and pipeline gas controller before confirmation cutoffs.

### Agent 4: Counterparty Credit Exposure & Tariff Default Sieve
* **In One Line**: Continuously monitors shipper credit exposure across forward transportation liabilities, outstanding imbalance cash-out balances, and collateral coverage against open-access tariff creditworthiness rules.
* **Friction Solved**: Eliminates 9.0 hours per month of manual accounts receivable aging cross-referencing, credit rating monitoring, and tariff collateral adequacy calculations.
* **The Specification**:
  * **Reads**: Daily shipper billed and unbilled accounts receivable aging, mark-to-market physical imbalance positions, three-month forward reservation fee obligations, letters of credit (LOC) and parental guarantees in the collateral vault, and credit rating agency feeds (S&P, Moody's, Fitch).
  * **Does**: Calculates total credit exposure for each active shipper as the sum of outstanding transportation invoices, unbilled mid-month gas deliveries, mark-to-market cumulative imbalance liabilities, and 3-month forward reservation charge obligations per tariff formula; compares total exposure against approved unsecured credit limits and posted collateral (LOCs, cash deposits); tracks letter of credit expiration dates with 30/60/90-day warning triggers; monitors credit agency rating downgrades below investment-grade thresholds; calculates additional collateral call demands required under tariff terms.
  * **Returns**: Shipper Credit Exposure & Margin Call Dossier showing credit headroom/deficit per shipper, expiring collateral watchlists, rating downgrade impact reports, and drafted tariff-compliant demand letters.
  * **Stops At**: Issuing formal statutory notices of contract suspension, calling bank letters of credit, or terminating transportation service.
* **Failure Modes & Safety Envelopes**: If a shipper's uncollateralized exposure exceeds their allowable tariff credit limit or if an active Letter of Credit falls within 15 calendar days of expiration without renewal, the agent flags `[Urgent Tariff Credit Default Risk: Collateral Demand Required]` and prepares a high-priority commercial risk escalation package.

* **The Specification**:
  * **Reads**: Monthly validated custody transfer meter volumes from measurement systems (Flow-Cal/PGAS), confirmed scheduled quantities from transportation management systems, contractual fuel retention deductions, inter-shipper imbalance trade logs, and published spot price indices (Gas Daily, Platts, Henry Hub, regional trading hubs).
  * **Does**: Calculates net monthly imbalance for each shipper as Total Scheduled Deliveries plus Fuel minus Total Allocated Receipts; accounts for authorized inter-shipper imbalance trades completed during the post-month trading window; evaluates final imbalance percentage relative to total scheduled deliveries; maps imbalance percentages into tariff-mandated tiered cash-out penalty brackets (e.g., 0%–5% at 100% index price; >5%–10% at 110%/90%; >10%–15% at 120%/80%; >20% at 150%/50%); applies appropriate regional hub daily high/low index prices to determine financial settlement amounts; audits Operational Balancing Agreements (OBAs) at pipeline interconnects.
  * **Returns**: Monthly Imbalance Cash-Out Settlement Dossier detailing meter-by-meter allocations, post-trade net imbalance volumes, tiered settlement calculations, and billing credit/debit files formatted for general ledger ingestion.
  * **Stops At**: Posting financial journal entries or transmitting final billing invoices to shippers without commercial analyst authorization.
* **Failure Modes & Safety Envelopes**: If allocated physical meter receipts at an interconnect disagree with scheduled volumes by more than the allowable Operational Balancing Agreement (OBA) tolerance threshold (e.g., > 2.0% cumulative linepack drift), the agent logs `[OBA Interconnect Variance Exceeded: Upstream Measurement Audit Required]` and quarantines cash-out processing for that interconnect point.

### Agent 6: System Fuel Retention & In-Kind Reimbursement Sieve
* **In One Line**: Audits monthly pipeline compression fuel burned, gas-fired auxiliary consumption, and unaccounted-for (UAF) lost gas against contractual in-kind shipper fuel retention recoveries to prevent commercial fuel under-recovery.
* **Friction Solved**: Eliminates 8.5 hours per month of manual compressor telemetry harvesting, fuel allocation cross-checking, and in-kind gas inventory reconciliation.
* **The Specification**:
  * **Reads**: SCADA and flow computer fuel gas meter data at pipeline compressor stations, electric motor-driven compressor power consumption telemetry, heating value and gas composition chromatography logs, scheduled shipper gas receipts, and contractual fuel retention percentage schedules.
  * **Does**: Computes total system fuel consumption (measured in Dth or GJ) across all compressor units, dehydration reboilers, and heater stations; aggregates total in-kind fuel gas retained from shippers based on contractually applied fuel retention percentages; calculates physical Unaccounted-For (UAF) gas volume by balancing total system receipts, deliveries, compressor fuel burn, and linepack changes; evaluates fuel recovery position (over-recovery or under-recovery); tracks fuel tracking tracker account balances against tariff true-up provisions; computes recommended semi-annual or annual tariff fuel retention rate adjustments.
  * **Returns**: Pipeline System Fuel & UAF Balance Dossier with compressor-by-compressor fuel burn breakdowns, shipper in-kind recovery variance waterfall charts, UAF percentage trends, and regulatory fuel tracker adjustment filings.
  * **Stops At**: Modifying filed tariff fuel retention percentage schedules or altering physical compressor station fuel regulator setpoints.
* **Failure Modes & Safety Envelopes**: If cumulative unaccounted-for (UAF) loss across the pipeline system exceeds tariff-mandated threshold limits (e.g., > 0.5% of total throughput) in a billing cycle, the agent issues `[Excessive UAF Gas Loss: Measurement Error or Unreported Line Leak Suspected]` and alerts both commercial contracts and pipeline integrity teams.

### Agent 7: Regulatory Index of Customers & Tariff Compliance Synthesizer
* **In One Line**: Synthesizes active transportation contract terms, customer capacity entitlements, discounted rate agreements, and operational service parameters into certified quarterly regulatory filings (FERC Form 549B and open-access disclosures).
* **Friction Solved**: Eliminates 11.5 hours per quarter of labor-intensive cross-database extraction, customer portfolio reconciliation, and regulatory XML/tabular dossier compilation.
* **The Specification**:

* **Failure Modes & Safety Envelopes**: If an active transportation agreement contains a negotiated rate or discount that lacks corresponding public tariff authority or board approval reference, the agent flags `[Unsubstantiated Tariff Discount: Regulatory Audit Risk]` and excludes the contract from final submission until commercial regulatory counsel verifies the legal tariff reference.

## 7 · Lines Worth Remembering & Open Questions

### The Line
> *"A pipeline company doesn't lose money on pipeline hydraulics or compressor engine trips; it loses money when a bankrupt shipper quietly builds up a thirty-day imbalance deficit while leaning on firm capacity rights that expired three weeks ago."*

### Authentic Open Questions for Midstream Commercial & Contract Practitioners
1. **Contract Ingestion Latency & Rate Accuracy**: When commercial business development executes a complex, negotiated-rate firm transportation agreement with non-standard fuel tiers and receipt-delivery path rights, how many business days elapse before all tariff parameters are accurately reflected in your transportation management system, and how often do post-effective-date billing adjustments occur?
2. **Real-Time Shipper Credit Exposure Blind Spots**: During periods of extreme regional spot gas price volatility, does your commercial team have automated, daily visibility into the combined credit exposure of each shipper—incorporating mark-to-market unbilled imbalances, forward 3-month reservation liabilities, and expiring letters of credit—or do credit deficits only surface during end-of-month accounting cash-outs?
3. **Fuel Retention Under-Recovery & UAF Tracking**: How closely is your contractual in-kind shipper fuel retention percentage reconciled against actual physical compressor fuel consumption and system linepack drift, and what is your annual unrecovered fuel cost variance due to stale tariff fuel tracker filing adjustments?
