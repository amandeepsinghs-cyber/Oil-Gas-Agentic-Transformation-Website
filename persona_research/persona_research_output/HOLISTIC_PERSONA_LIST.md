# Section 1: Subsurface & Geosciences

### 1. Petrophysicist `[CORE 20]`
* **Canonical Title**: Petrophysicist
* **Common Aliases**: Formation Evaluation Specialist, Log Analyst
* **Operational Setting**: Technical Center / Asset Team Office
* **Work Cadence**: Project-based and drilling campaign support (on-call during logging runs)
* **Governing Competence Standards**: SPWLA Petrophysics Skill Set Guidelines (v2.0, 2022); SPE Formation Evaluation Competency Matrix
* **Primary Ownership**: Well log interpretation, borehole environmental corrections, petrophysical cutoffs (porosity $\phi$, water saturation $S_w$, shale volume $V_{sh}$, net-to-gross), core-to-log integration.
* **Core Manual Friction**: Splicing disparate vintage log runs across decades of acquisitions (LAS, DLIS, TIFF formats), hand-aligning depth shifts, and removing bad-hole intervals caused by caliper washouts.
* **Candidate Agent Focus**: **Well Log Splice & Normalization Agent**
  * *Reads*: Raw multi-run LAS/DLIS logs, drilling bit records, and mud logs.
  * *Does*: Cross-correlates gamma ray/resistivity curves across overlapping runs, identifies caliper washouts, and executes curve alignment.
  * *Returns*: Spliced, depth-matched composite curves with flag logs indicating edited intervals for human approval.
  * *Stops at*: Final petrophysical cutoff determination and saturation exponent selection.

### 2. Petroleum Geologist `[CORE 20]`
* **Canonical Title**: Petroleum Geologist
* **Common Aliases**: Explorationist, Exploration Geologist *(avoiding mining title confusion)*, Basin Modeler
* **Operational Setting**: Regional Exploration / New Ventures Office
* **Work Cadence**: Long-cycle prospect generation, exploration licensing rounds, appraisal framing
* **Governing Competence Standards**: AAPG Competency Framework; SPE Subsurface Discipline Matrix
* **Primary Ownership**: Regional basin architecture, petroleum systems analysis (source rock maturity, migration pathways, trapping mechanisms, seal integrity), play fairway mapping, prospect risking.
* **Core Manual Friction**: Excavating unstructured regional data: transcribing formation tops from hundreds of scanned 1970s–1990s completion cards, scout tickets, and paper mudlogs into a modern database.
* **Candidate Agent Focus**: **Legacy Well Data Archaeology Agent**
  * *Reads*: Scanned well completion reports, historical scout tickets, and paper mudlog PDFs.
  * *Does*: Extracts formation top picks, reported hydrocarbon shows, and DST (drill stem test) flow rates.
  * *Returns*: Structured stratigraphic database with confidence scores and source document deep links.
  * *Stops at*: Regional structural correlation and trap definition.

  * *Stops at*: Structural horizon picking and seismic fault interpretation.

  * *Stops at*: Official reserves categorization and field abandonment pressure selection.

  * *Returns*: Offset risk matrix and suggested casing seat depth windows.
  * *Stops at*: Final casing wall thickness selection and approval of the well program.
