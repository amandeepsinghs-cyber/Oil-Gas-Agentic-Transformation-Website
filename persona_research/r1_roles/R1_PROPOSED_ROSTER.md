> **Status:** PROPOSAL, awaiting approval. Generated from 18 Flash sub-segment research agents (`r1_raw.json`) and Lead synthesis.

## Bottom line
**Add 20 well-known roles: 9 Midstream and 11 Downstream.** That takes the system from 33 to **53 personas**. Each domain tab then reads as a full, recognisable workforce without clutter. 71 raw candidates were narrowed to 20 by merging duplicates, folding specialisms into the role people know, and dropping manual-only roles.
