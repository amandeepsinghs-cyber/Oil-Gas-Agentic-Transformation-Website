## 2. Master Storytelling & Editorial Web Principles (The Apple Standard)

### A. Total Eradication of Slide-Deck Tropes
* **BANNED: The Box Trap.** Never enclose text, metrics, or concepts inside bordered `.glass-panel` cards or rigid boxes. Putting borders around every paragraph turns an executive website into a grid of slide handouts.
* **BANNED: The Frame Trap.** Never trap 3D renders, architectural diagrams, or reservoir curves inside bordered rectangular frames or screenshot boxes. Visuals must breathe and blend seamlessly into the obsidian canvas.
* **BANNED: Slide HUD Clutter.** Zero artificial slide counters (`01 / 15`, `Slide 1 of 5`), zero artificial stage badges, zero click-to-advance presentation decks.
* **BANNED: Fixed Dimensions & Scaling Hacks.** Never use fixed `1920x1080` canvases or CSS `transform: scale(...)`. The viewport is fluid, responsive, and natural.
* **MANDATED: Pure Scrollytelling.** Continuous, full-bleed vertical narrative progression with generous breathing room (`padding: 100px max(24px, 4vw)`).

### B. The Billboard Rule (3-Second Executive Truth)
An Energy CMD or CEO does not read bulleted cards. They absorb an undeniable truth in three seconds:
* **Lead Display Headline:** Massive, sculptural typography (`clamp(38px, 5.2vw, 76px)`), weight `800`, letter-spacing `-2px` to `-2.5px`. It delivers the core revelation immediately.
* **Secondary Voice:** A single, sharp, elegant sentence in muted gray (`#94A3B8`) that delivers the punchline with intellectual gravitas.
* **Typographic Hierarchy Over Boxes:** Grouping and priority are achieved strictly through font scale, weight contrast, and generous negative space—never through borders and backgrounds.

### C. Heroic Data (Big Numbers as Sculptural Monuments)
Capital exposure in energy is measured in hundreds of crores. Never hide these numbers inside small table cells or card corners:
* Stat values must be **monumental**: `clamp(48px, 5.5vw, 84px)`, weight `800`, line-height `1`, letter-spacing `-1.5px`.
* The metric floats directly on the canvas as a piece of sculptural typography.
* Beneath the number: a quiet, high-conviction 1-line label (12px–13px uppercase monospace) and a single sentence of contextual narrative.
* Generous negative space between metrics creates dramatic tension.

### D. Unconfined, Floating Visuals
* High-resolution 3D renders, subterranean geological cross-sections, and architectural flows float **freely in space**.
* Edges are softly vignetted to blend directly into the obsidian void (`#06090E`).
* Annotations and callouts are minimal, razor-sharp typographic anchors positioned directly near the visual elements—never disconnected into separate cards or tables.

### E. The "Surgical Plug" Conceptual Paradigm
* **An Agent is a Surgical Plug** (like an oilfield bridge plug, isolation packer, or cement squeeze).
* Banned analogies: "sand" or "marbles" (too abstract, passive, and vague).
* **The Systems-of-Record Guarantee:** We do not replace your cheese. Existing platforms of record (**Petrel, Techlog, OpenWorks, Honeywell Experion, SAP**) are preserved and respected.
* Agents deploy as autonomous, deterministic sentinels directly into the unmonitored human handovers between disciplines, permanently sealing the latent seams where capital bleeds.

## 5. Front-End Engineering & Interaction Standards

1. **Fluid Web Architecture:**
   * Pure modern HTML5, CSS3, and ES6+ vanilla JavaScript.
   * Native CSS Grid and Flexbox layouts that expand organically.
   * Absolutely zero transform-scale wrappers. `#viewport-scaler` has `transform: none !important`.
2. **Apple-Calibrated Typography Stack:**
   * Display Headline: `"Google Sans", -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`.
   * Technical / Telemetry / Code: `"JetBrains Mono", "Roboto Mono", Consolas, monospace`.
3. **Smooth Scrollspy Navigation:**
   * Sticky top header with frosted glass backdrop (`backdrop-filter: blur(20px); background: rgba(6, 9, 14, 0.82)`).
   * Smooth chapter jumps (`#stage-XX`) with active viewport observer synchronization.
   * Floating bottom dock pill displaying current narrative stage with smooth prev/next controls and fullscreen toggle.
4. **Performance & GPU Acceleration:**
   * Ultra-light footprint with sub-second page load.
   * Animated SVGs and canvas graphs use GPU-friendly properties (`transform`, `opacity`, `stroke-dashoffset`).
   * 60fps rendering without jank or scroll hitching.
